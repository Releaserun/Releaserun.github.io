About ReleaseRun

ReleaseRun tracks software releases across the dev ecosystem. We cover 13+ core technologies including Node.js, React, TypeScript, Python, Go, Rust, Kubernetes, PostgreSQL, and more.

Our tools are built for engineering teams who need to understand release risk before upgrading. Everything is free, most tools run client-side, and no signup is required.

We also offer a CLI tool that scans your dependency files for EOL versions and CVE risk directly from your terminal.
